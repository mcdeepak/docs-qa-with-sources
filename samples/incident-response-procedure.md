# Incident Response Procedure

**Owner:** Security  |  **Version:** 1.2

## 1. Severity levels
| Level | Description | First response |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Critical outage or confirmed data breach | 15 minutes |
| P2 | Major degradation affecting many users | 1 hour |
| P3 | Limited impact, workaround available | 1 business day |
| P4 | Minor issue or request | 3 business days |

## 2. Reporting
- Any suspected security incident must be reported to the service desk within 1 hour of discovery.
- If personal data may be involved, the Privacy Officer must be notified within 24 hours.

## 3. After the incident
- A post-incident review is held within 5 business days for every P1 and P2.
