# Device and Laptop Policy

**Owner:** IT Operations  |  **Version:** 2.0

## 1. Company laptops
- Laptops are refreshed every 4 years.
- All laptops must have full-disk encryption (BitLocker) enabled. Devices without it must not be issued.

## 2. Personal devices (BYOD)
- Personal phones and tablets may be used for work email only, through the managed email app enrolled in Intune.
- Personal devices must not be used to store company files locally.

## 3. Removable media
- USB storage is blocked by default.
- Exceptions require approval from the Security team and are valid for 30 days.
