# Onboarding Checklist

**Owner:** People and Culture  |  **Version:** 1.0

## Before the first day
1. The hiring manager orders equipment at least 5 business days before the start date.
2. The hiring manager assigns an onboarding buddy.

## First week
1. The new starter completes security awareness training within 5 days of starting.
2. The new starter enrols in multi-factor authentication on the first day.
3. The new starter reads and acknowledges the Acceptable Use Policy.

## First month
1. The hiring manager sets probation goals within 30 days.
