# Software and Licensing Policy

**Owner:** IT Operations  |  **Version:** 1.1

## 1. Requesting software
- New software is requested through the service desk catalogue.
- Requests are assessed within 5 business days.

## 2. Open source
- Open source libraries with a copyleft licence need approval from Legal before use in a product.
- Permissive licences such as MIT and Apache 2.0 do not need approval.

## 3. Licence compliance
- A licence audit is carried out every 6 months.
- Unused licences are reclaimed after 90 days without sign-in.
