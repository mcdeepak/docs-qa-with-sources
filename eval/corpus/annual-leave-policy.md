# Annual Leave Policy

**Owner:** People and Culture  |  **Version:** 4.1

## 1. Entitlement
- Full-time employees accrue 20 days of annual leave per year.
- Part-time employees accrue annual leave in proportion to their contracted hours.

## 2. Requesting leave
- Annual leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 2 weeks before the first day of leave.
- Requests for more than 10 consecutive working days need approval from the department head.

## 3. Carry-over
- Up to 5 days of unused annual leave may be carried over into the next year.
- Carried-over days expire on 31 March.

## 4. Public holidays
- Public holidays are not deducted from the annual leave balance.
