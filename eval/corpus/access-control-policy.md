# Access Control Policy

**Owner:** Security  |  **Version:** 1.6

## 1. Principles
- Access is granted on the principle of least privilege.
- Every access request must be approved by the system owner.

## 2. Joiners, movers and leavers
- Accounts for new starters are created no earlier than 3 business days before the start date.
- When a person leaves the company, their accounts are disabled within 4 hours of their last shift.
- When a person changes role, their previous access is removed within 5 business days.

## 3. Access reviews
- System owners review user access every quarter.
- Administrator access is reviewed every month.

## 4. Shared and service accounts
- Shared accounts are not permitted for interactive use.
- Service account credentials are rotated every 180 days.
