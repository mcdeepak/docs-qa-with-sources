# Email and Messaging Policy

**Owner:** IT Governance  |  **Version:** 1.8

## 1. Email
- Automatic forwarding of company email to a personal mailbox is prohibited.
- The maximum attachment size is 25 MB. Larger files are shared through the document portal.

## 2. Phishing
- Suspicious emails are reported with the Report Phishing button in the mail client.
- Do not click links or open attachments in a suspicious email.

## 3. Chat
- Chat messages are retained for 2 years.
- Confidential information must not be posted in public channels.
