# Data Classification Standard

**Owner:** Security  |  **Version:** 2.4

## 1. Classification levels

| Classification | Meaning | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Approved for release outside the company | No restrictions |
| Internal | For staff only | Must not be shared outside the company without approval |
| Confidential | Sensitive business or customer information | Encrypted in transit and at rest |
| Restricted | Highly sensitive, such as credentials and keys | Encrypted, access logged, need-to-know only |

## 2. Labelling
- Documents classified Confidential or Restricted must carry a label in the header.

## 3. Disposal
- Paper documents classified Confidential or above are disposed of in the locked shredding bins.
