# Change Management Procedure

**Owner:** IT Operations  |  **Version:** 2.0

## 1. Change types

| Type | Approval | Lead time |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | Pre-approved, no review needed | None |
| Normal | Change Advisory Board | 5 business days |
| Emergency | On-call manager | None, reviewed afterwards |

## 2. Change Advisory Board
- The Change Advisory Board meets every Tuesday at 10 am.
- A change request must include a rollback plan and a test plan.

## 3. Freeze periods
- No normal changes are made to production between 15 December and 10 January.

## 4. Emergency changes
- Every emergency change is reviewed at the next Change Advisory Board meeting.
