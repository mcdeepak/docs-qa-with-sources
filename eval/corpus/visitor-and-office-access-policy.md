# Visitor and Office Access Policy

**Owner:** Facilities  |  **Version:** 2.2

## 1. Visitors
- All visitors must sign in at reception and wear a visitor badge.
- Visitors must be escorted by their host at all times.
- Visitor badges must be returned when leaving the building.

## 2. Access cards
- A lost access card must be reported to Facilities within 24 hours.
- Access cards must not be lent to another person.

## 3. Office hours
- The office is open from 7 am to 7 pm on weekdays.
- After-hours access requires approval from the line manager.
