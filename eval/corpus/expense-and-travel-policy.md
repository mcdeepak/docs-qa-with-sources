# Expense and Travel Policy

**Owner:** Finance  |  **Version:** 3.2

## 1. Claiming expenses
- Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase.
- A receipt is required for every expense above 30 dollars.
- Claims are reimbursed in the next fortnightly pay run after approval.

## 2. Daily limits

| Expense type | Limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Meals while travelling | 85 dollars per day | Alcohol is not reimbursed |
| Hotel, capital cities | 280 dollars per night | Book through the travel portal |
| Hotel, regional | 190 dollars per night | Book through the travel portal |
| Taxi or rideshare | 60 dollars per trip | Use public transport where practical |

## 3. Flights
- Flights shorter than 6 hours must be booked in economy class.
- Flights must be booked at least 14 days before departure unless the trip is urgent.

## 4. Corporate cards
- Corporate cards must not be used for personal purchases.
- A lost corporate card must be reported to Finance immediately so it can be cancelled.
