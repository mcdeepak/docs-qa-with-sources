# Acceptable Use Policy

**Owner:** IT Governance  |  **Version:** 2.3

## 1. General use
Company systems are provided for business purposes. Limited personal use is acceptable provided it does not interfere with work or breach this policy.

## 2. Prohibited activities
- Installing unlicensed software on a company device is prohibited.
- Company systems must not be used for cryptocurrency mining.
- Sharing an account or password with another person is prohibited.

## 3. Monitoring
- Network traffic and system activity are logged and may be reviewed by the Security team.
- Monitoring is carried out in line with the Workplace Surveillance Notice.

## 4. Breaches
- Suspected breaches of this policy are investigated by Security and People and Culture.
