# Vendor and Procurement Policy

**Owner:** Finance  |  **Version:** 2.1

## 1. Purchase approval

| Purchase value | Approver | Quotes needed |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 2,000 dollars | Line manager | 1 |
| 2,001 to 10,000 dollars | Department head | 2 |
| Above 10,000 dollars | Chief Financial Officer | 3 |

## 2. Vendor security
- A vendor that will handle company data must pass a security assessment before a contract is signed.
- Vendor security assessments are repeated every 2 years.

## 3. Contracts
- Contracts longer than 12 months are reviewed by Legal.
